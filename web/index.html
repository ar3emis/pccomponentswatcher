Where the numbers come from

Every price is read live from the retailer's own listing page, per product variant, by a scheduled job that runs every six hours. Only in-stock items are published. A failed source is reported here rather than hidden — the site never invents a price to fill a gap.

About this page

Prices are converted to INR using live mid-market rates and are indicative only — they exclude shipping, duties and local taxes, and can change between refreshes. Always confirm on the retailer's own page before buying. Product names link straight to the source listing.
